Process

    It starts with a simple thought: what would make this great?

  2. Design

    Sketches show how it should look and feel.

About the studio

A small studio with the whole package

Falch Studios is an independent digital studio in Denmark. Idea, design, code, art and sound are made in the same place – so the path from thought to finished product is short.

Modern AI tools are a fixed part of the workshop. The idea, the design and the final decisions are always made by a human.

Right now the studio is working on Kingdom Defense: Sealed Worlds, which is in closed testing on Google Play.

The studio's principles

  • No countdowns and no pressure to buy.
  • Honest about ads and in-app purchases.
  • Images made with AI are labeled as AI.
  • No false promises – only what the product can actually do.
