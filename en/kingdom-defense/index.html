Sealed Worlds

20 sealed worlds. Monster bosses. 20 insane guns. How far can you hold the line?

A tower defense game for one hand and short sessions. Your bunker is the last thing standing, and something new is coming down the road. Your gun fires by itself – you decide what to build, where to set your traps and when to call in the jets.

The game

What's waiting for you

  • 20 sealed worlds

    From burning forests to the deep sea and from a neon city to the edge of a black hole. Each world stays sealed until you clear the one before it – or break the seal yourself.

  • Bosses that fight back

    Giant machines and monsters weave across the road, dodge your fire, shell your base, phase out of reach and call for backup.

  • 20 main guns

    Each wilder than the last: Gatling cannons, rail guns, rivers of fire, chain lightning – and a gun that opens a black hole.

  • Call in the jets

    Tap AIRSTRIKE and the pilot answers over the radio. You hear the jets long before you see them, and the battle slows down while they roar overhead.

  • Every world has its own rules

    Fog that hides the enemy, sandstorms that blind your guns, lightning, eruptions, hackers and low gravity.

  • Build your defense

    Flak, tesla, cryo and mortar towers, plus mines, spikes, barricades and EMP traps.

  • Always something more

    Treasure convoys to hunt down, supply drops to grab, a daily mission, endless mode, ranks from recruit to living legend, medals and stars.

  • Built for one hand

    Made for short sessions. No account needed, and the game works offline.
